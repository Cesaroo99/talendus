What we see

Companies do not all hire the same way. Skills, experience, contract type and location weigh as much as the resume. A seat left vacant too long often costs more than a well-scoped recruiting mandate.

Practical moves

  • Clarify the role, real pay and required skills before approaching the market.
  • Assess know-how (demonstration, scenarios) rather than diplomas alone.
  • Plan 30/60/90-day onboarding: that is where retention is won.

How Talendus steps in

We target profiles by role and skills, validate the fit and present a few files, each one we're ready to stand behind. Talendus already uses AI internally to analyse information faster and spot correspondences; it does not choose in the company's place. Qualification stays human.
